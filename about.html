About the project

DealGuard

An Intelligent E-Commerce Deal Verification and Dark Pattern Detection System.

DealGuard is an academic prototype designed to help users review online deal information. It checks whether the advertised discount is close to the discount calculated from entered prices, optionally compares the selling price with a supplied category reference dataset, and records three shopping-interface signals: countdown timers, low-stock warnings and hidden checkout charges.

Limitations: product data is entered by the user; external shopping sites may restrict automated access. The rule-based score is an indicative project metric, not a certified fraud detector.
